Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2024

Felix is built with your privacy as a priority. We collect only what is necessary to deliver the app's features, and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

Felix collects information you provide directly when using the app:

  • Cat profile data — name, breed, age, weight, and photos you add
  • Health records — wellness logs, vet appointments, medications, vaccinations
  • Usage data — how you interact with features to improve the app
  • Photos — photos you add to the photo journal, stored locally on your device

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and improve the Felix app experience
  • Power AI features via your Anthropic API connection
  • Send feeding and health reminders you have configured
  • Analyse food products via the Open Food Facts database

3. Data Storage

All your cat's data — including health records, photos, and logs — is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage. We do not upload your personal data to external servers.

When you use AI features (Mood Lab, AI Assistant), your input is sent to Anthropic's API via a secure proxy. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this data. We do not store AI conversation history on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Felix uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API — for AI-powered features. Data sent is limited to the context needed for each analysis. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Open Food Facts — for food product lookups via barcode scan. Only the barcode number is sent. See Open Food Facts Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

Felix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete the app to remove all data
  • Clear individual records within the app at any time
  • Revoke notification permissions via iPhone Settings
  • Revoke camera/photo permissions via iPhone Settings

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@felix.app.
