Common Questions

🔄
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to iPhone Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Felix → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
📱
My data disappeared after update
All data is saved locally on your device. If data is missing, it may be a first-launch issue. Contact us and we'll help restore it.
🤖
AI features not working
AI features require an internet connection. Check your connection. If the issue persists, the daily limit of 2 Mood Lab analyses may have been reached.
📷
Camera or photo access issues
Go to iPhone Settings → Felix → and make sure Camera and Photos permissions are enabled.
🔔
Not receiving reminders
Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Felix and make sure notifications are allowed. Also check that reminders are enabled inside the app.
🐱
Can I use Felix for multiple cats?
Yes! Go to Settings → My Cats → Add New to add multiple cats. Switch between them using the bar at the top of the screen.

When emailing support, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • A description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • Any screenshots if relevant
